🛡️ Security Architecture & Ban Isolation System

🔒 Anti-Ban Collateral Isolation

Every forwarded player connection is cryptographically bound to an isolated loopback IP in the 127.10.x.y - 127.249.x.y range. Banning a player with /ban-ip bans strictly their identity without ever locking out 127.0.0.1 or other players.

🛡️ Perimeter HMAC Anti-Spoofing

IP allocations are computed on the secure relay edge via HMAC-SHA256 from the verified physical TCP socket. Clients have zero capability to forge or impersonate other player addresses.

⚡ DDoS & Flood Defense

Features strict 40/min handshake rate-limiting, 6-socket concurrency caps per IP, 4.5s Slowloris mitigation timeouts, and 2MB frame caps.
